Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026
Operator: SERN, Republic of Korea
Contact: kicoa24@gmail.com

SERN (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the glowie iOS app. This Privacy Policy explains what information glowie handles when you edit or create photos, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we handle

  • Photos and instructions you choose: glowie accesses only photos you select through the iOS photo picker or take with the camera. If you use an AI effect or template, we process the selected photo, your chosen effect or template, and any optional instruction you enter, such as a background-cleanup note. Photos can contain faces or other personal information; please choose images you are allowed to use.
  • Generated and edited images: glowie receives the resulting image and may keep it in the app’s local gallery on your device. When you choose “Save to Photos,” iOS adds the image to your photo library with your permission.
  • Anonymous service identifier: glowie uses Supabase anonymous authentication to protect AI requests. This creates a technical user ID without asking you for an email address or password. Session tokens are stored in your device’s Keychain. Supabase may process related authentication and security information, such as IP address, device or request information, and sign-in logs.
  • Purchases and preferences: Apple handles subscription and credit-pack purchases and payments. RevenueCat processes purchase records, anonymous purchase identifiers, subscription entitlements and credit balances for glowie. We do not receive your payment-card details. App preferences, such as language and your one-time AI-photo consent choice, are stored on your device.
  • Messages you send us: if you email us, we receive the information you include so we can respond.

2. How photos are processed

Filters and beauty adjustments available in the on-device editor run on your device. A selected photo is sent for AI generation only when you choose to create an AI result after accepting the in-app notice. The app sends the photo and related request through SERN’s Supabase Edge Function to OpenAI’s image API and receives the generated image in the response. The app and Edge Function do not intentionally save uploaded photos or AI results in a server-side photo library. Photos are processed in memory for the request. Service providers may keep security or abuse-monitoring records under their own policies; OpenAI’s standard API abuse-monitoring logs may be retained for up to 30 days, or longer where required for legal or safety reasons. By default, OpenAI does not use API inputs and outputs to train its models unless the API customer opts in.

3. Why we use information

We use this information to provide editing and AI creation, keep the app’s local gallery working, validate requests and subscriptions, prevent misuse, troubleshoot failures, answer support requests, and comply with legal obligations. Where applicable, we rely on your request to provide the service, your choice to submit a photo for AI processing, our legitimate interest in protecting the service, and legal obligations.

4. Who receives information

We share information only as needed to operate glowie: Supabase provides anonymous authentication and the Edge Function; OpenAI processes AI image requests; RevenueCat manages purchase entitlements and credit balances; Apple provides photo-library permissions and in-app purchase services. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not sell personal information or use third-party advertising trackers in the app. We do not publish your photos or make your app gallery public.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Images in glowie’s gallery are stored on your device until you delete them in the app or remove the app. Images saved to Apple Photos remain there until you remove them from Photos. The AI request is not stored as a permanent photo record by SERN’s Edge Function, but Supabase and OpenAI may retain operational or security records under their policies. Supabase anonymous authentication records can remain until deleted; uninstalling glowie does not automatically delete them. Support emails are retained as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

You can delete individual images from glowie’s gallery, remove images from Apple Photos, or uninstall glowie to remove its local data. You can stop future AI photo transfers by not using AI creation. To request access to or deletion of information held by SERN, including an anonymous authentication record where we can identify it, email kicoa24@gmail.com. We may need enough information to locate the relevant record and verify the request. We will respond as required by applicable law.

6. Security and international processing

We use HTTPS for network requests and the iOS Keychain for session tokens. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Supabase, OpenAI, and Apple may process information outside the Republic of Korea, subject to their service arrangements and applicable safeguards.

7. Children

glowie is not directed to children under 13 or any higher minimum age required by local law. Do not submit a child’s photo unless you have the authority to do so. If you believe a child’s information was submitted improperly, contact us.

8. Your choices and policy changes

You control which photos you select, whether to use AI creation, and whether to allow camera or photo-library access in iOS Settings. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings. We may update this policy as the app changes. The revised policy will show a new effective date in the app; we will provide additional notice where required by law.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact SERN at kicoa24@gmail.com.
