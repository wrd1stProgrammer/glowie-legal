Support

Need help with glowie, a purchase or your data? Email kicoa24@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Do not email private photos unless you choose to share them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. If an active subscription is not showing in glowie, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.

Photos and AI

Check camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. AI creation needs an internet connection. If a result fails, try again or use another photo.

Privacy requests

Contact us at the email above to request help with data access or deletion. See our Privacy Policy.
